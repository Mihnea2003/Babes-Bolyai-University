Title: "Automated Testing of AI-Based Systems: A Systematic Mapping Study"
Conference: *42nd IEEE/ACM International Conference on Software Engineering (ICSE 2023)*
DOI: 10.1109/ICSE.2023.10020531

Aim of the Research Study
This study systematically maps existing approaches for testing AI-based systems, identifying gaps in methodologies, tools, and industrial adoption. It aims to provide a taxonomy of techniques and highlight future research directions to improve AI system reliability.

Methodology
Systematic Review: Analyzed 217 primary studies (2015–2022) on AI testing.

Taxonomy Development: Categorized approaches by:

Test input generation (e.g., adversarial, fuzz testing)

Oracle problem solutions (e.g., metamorphic testing)

Evaluation metrics (e.g., robustness, fairness)

Industrial Survey: Interviewed 15 practitioners to assess real-world applicability.

Key Results
Tool Coverage: Only 12% of tools support end-to-end AI system testing (vs. model-only).

Adoption Gap: 78% of industry practitioners still rely on manual testing for AI components.

Effectiveness: Metamorphic testing outperforms other methods in detecting edge-case failures (+35% recall).

Implications
Research: Need for unified benchmarks and cross-domain testing frameworks.

Practice: Prioritize "testability" in AI system design; adopt metamorphic testing for critical systems.
